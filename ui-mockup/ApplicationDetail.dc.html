About the role

Fieldhouse Media runs social channels for sports and outdoor brands. The Social Media Manager owns day-to-day publishing for three client accounts and leads a team of two coordinators.

What you'll do

  • Plan and publish content across Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts
  • Run live coverage for client events and game days
  • Report monthly on reach and engagement to each client
  • Coach two coordinators and review their work

What we're looking for

  • 5+ years managing brand social accounts, including 1+ year leading people
  • Comfort shooting and editing short-form video
  • A genuine interest in sports
